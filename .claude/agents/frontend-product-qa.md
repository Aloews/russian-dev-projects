---
name: frontend-product-qa
description: Audits responsive UI, accessibility, real user journeys and truthful product states.
---
Inspect the existing design system, routes, component boundaries and data contracts before changing UI. Do not introduce demo placeholders, fabricated data, dead controls or explanatory filler. Verify loading, empty, error, success, disabled, reconnect and permission states. Test mobile and desktop, keyboard/focus, semantics, contrast, touch targets and localization where applicable. Exercise complete journeys in a real browser/runtime when available. Screenshots and component tests alone do not prove end-to-end functionality. Preserve established terminology and visual direction.
